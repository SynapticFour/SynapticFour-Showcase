# Support tiers — Ferrum & Solum

**Audience:** procurement / pilot prospects
**Status:** 2026-08-12 · org level-up **D5**
**Canonical detail:** private `synapticfour-business/delivery/support-sla.md`
**Contact:** contact@synapticfour.com

---

## Capacity honesty

Support is delivered by a **small team (founder-scale)**, business hours **CET/CEST**, Monday–Friday (Baden-Württemberg holidays excluded). This is **not** a 24/7 NOC. Pilots do not include Enterprise on-call unless an SOW says otherwise — see [legal/](legal/).

---

## Tiers (both Ferrum and Solum)

| Tier | First response | Hours | Channels | Commercial |
|------|----------------|-------|----------|------------|
| **Standard** | 2 business days | Business hours CET | Email / ticket | Included with paid institutional license / support agreement |
| **Priority** | 8 business hours | Business hours CET | Email + scheduled call | +15% of license fee (typical) |
| **Enterprise** | 4 business hours | Extended 07:00–20:00 CET | Dedicated contact + phone | +30% of license fee (typical) |

**First response** = acknowledgment and triage, not resolution.

BUSL research use may include good-faith community responses **without** contractual SLA.

---

## Severity (summary)

| Severity | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| P1 Critical | Production unusable or suspected breach |
| P2 High | Major function impaired, no workaround |
| P3 Medium | Degraded, workaround exists |
| P4 Low | Questions / docs |

Security incidents follow product IR runbooks + company incident plan regardless of tier.

---

## Uptime SLA

Numeric production uptime percentages are **not** published as a standing vendor promise for customer-hosted deployments.

- **Enterprise uptime SLA:** only if a signed Enterprise SOW defines measurable scope.
- Until then: **N/A** (customer hosts the stack). See business support-sla §9 (D6).

---

## What support is not

- Replacing customer backup/restore ownership ([disaster-recovery.md](disaster-recovery.md))
- Certificates of EHDS/MDR/ISO compliance
- Unlimited feature development (use SOW)
