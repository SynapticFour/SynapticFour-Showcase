## Who you are

You prepare genomic (and linked clinical) data for **genomDE** workflows and/or deposit toward **GHGA** Genome Data Centers. You need GA4GH-aligned APIs, controlled access, and clear consent/purpose encoding **before** archive submission — without sending raw data to a vendor cloud by default.

## Pain

- Site-level metadata/consent inconsistency blocks secondary use
- Building a full GA4GH stack in-house is unrealistic
- Procurement asks for evidence and key custody answers, not slideware
